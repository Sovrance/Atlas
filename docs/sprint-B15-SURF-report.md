# Sprint B15-SURF — report

**Reproducing a DOMAIN-layer result is not evidence for the UNIVERSAL-layer conjecture.**
Every result in this sprint is a known perturbative-QFT identity (Tr φ³ zeros, splits, the
δ-shift to the NLSM) or a published EFT bound reproduced with the program's own verifier.
By SPEC §2 an encoding success at layer 2 is never, by itself, evidence for layer 1; nothing
here says anything about whether 𝕽 exists. What the sprint measures is the *machinery*:
whether the exact-PSD verifier, the fingerprint/identification stack and the certificate
discipline behave correctly on cases that have ground truth — the disanalogy the research
report names as the sharpest caveat (`ground_truth_route` is recorded on every certificate
for this reason).

not merged, not pushed. Work order: `archive/sprint-drops/gv-b15-surf.zip` (also filed as

## 3. Which GID outcome obtained

- **Primary correlator `b15.gid.jaccard_atoms_v1`** (pole, zero, split and δ-class atoms):
  **Outcome A.** At the held-out n = 8 all three classes are identified (`FORCED`:
  C0 → TrPhi3, C1 → NLSM, C2 → TrPhi3_shifted), similarity = confidence = 1 ≥ 9/10, δ recovered.
- **`b15.gid.invariants_only_v1`** (δ-class removed): NLSM is still singled out by its pole set,
  but Tr φ³ and its δ-deformation tie → `AMBIGUOUS([TrPhi3, TrPhi3_shifted])`. This is the
  two-hash design working as documented: δ-insensitive invariants cannot separate a base
  grammar from its deformation.
- **`b15.gid.zeros_only_v1`** (poles and δ removed): **Outcome B** —
  `OBSERVATIONALLY_EQUIVALENT([NLSM, TrPhi3, TrPhi3_shifted] mod δ-shift)` for every class.
  The theories share every hidden zero *by construction* (the δ-shift is c-preserving), so
  fingerprint-only identification from zeros is structurally limited for deformation-related
  grammars. This is the epistemic result the work order flagged as the more important one; it
  is filed as a result, not as a classifier failure.

The primary-correlator definition (inclusion of the δ-class atom) was fixed after a
development run showed that a pole/zero/split-only Jaccard cannot separate a grammar from
its own deformation; it was written into prereg-002 before the certified run, and the
invariants-only correlator is retained so that the earlier behaviour remains visible.

**Redundancy metric (P1, recorded not acted on):** `C_{n−2} / #fingerprint atoms` =
5/12, 2/3, 132/43 (Tr φ³, n = 5, 6, 8); 14/15, 132/31 (NLSM, n = 6, 8).
